Yoa

 

Major Appearances: Attack of the Saucer Morons -- Mysterious Mysteries

Quote: "And, like the alien said, 'go to the Blotch! The Blotch knows,' so I like left my husband and came out here to commune with the Blotch!"

Affiliation: Children of the Bright and Shinning Saucer

See also: The Blotch, Trudy, Desmond Flapp, Boll, Starchunk

This member of the Children of the Bright and Shinning Saucer is a pimply teenage girl. She wants to be filled with Zim's wisdom and when Zim escapes on the voot carrier pig, she clings onto it until Zim knocks her off into a lake. Yoa later leaves her husband to go meet the Blotch and get her questions answered.

Yolk

 

Major Appearances: The Halloween Spectacular of Spooky Doom

Quote: "He said we could destroy his friend. Then he threw a can at my head. It hurt."

Affiliation: Nightmare World

See also: Nightmare Monsters, Nightmare Bitters

When Hummelflesh brought Zim back instead of Dib, Nightmare Bitters sent Yolk to announce that if Dib didn't surrender, Zim would be destroyed. Dib didn't care and he threw a can at Yolk's head. Yolk went back to Nightmare Bitters and due to his failure he was condemned to the realm of eternal screaming and restlessness.
