Walton Chunky

 

Major Appearances: Bad, Bad Rubber Piggy

Quote: none

Affiliation: Breakfast Chunks

See also: Horrible Giant Squid

Walton Chunky is the creator of the successful Breakfast Chunks cereal. He came up with the idea for it while working at a dump. Professor Membrane shows a possible reality in which Walton Chunky never invented Breakfast Chunks and instead was attacked by a horrible giant squid.

White Coats

 

Major Appearances: The Halloween Spectacular of Spooky Doom

Quote: "If there's one thing we understand, son, it's insanity."

Affiliation: Crazy House for Boys

See also: Nightmare White Coats

Dispatch units for the Crazy House for Boys, the white coats can be summoned to the skool instantly by use of the crazy cards. A pair of white coats named Chuck and Buck (see their name tags) arrive in Ms. Bitters' class after she places a crazy collar on Dib's neck. They take Dib to the Crazy House for Boys, while he jumps in and out of reality.
