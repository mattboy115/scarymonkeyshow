Santa Suit

 

Major Appearances: The Most Horrible X-Mas Ever

Quote: "Malfunction overload!"

Affiliation: Zim- experiment

See also: Mall Santa, Cutest Kid

The Santa suit is one of Zim's inventions, developed from a Vortian liquid substance. Each molecule contains a microscopic hard drive full of Santa data.

 

 Zim controlled the suit with a brain interface panel attached to his head. The suit began to think it was the real Santa and every time Zim encountered something jolly the suit took over and acted jolly itself. Zim inserted a stabilizer into the suit to keep it under control until he later saw an excruciatingly cute child. The stabilizer flew out and the suit took permanent control. Dib blasted the suit with a mech and the suit went crazy. It mutated into a horrible creature and attacked Dib. Dib finally blasted enough missiles into it so that it weakened, allowing Zim to escape the suit. Zim used the brain interface to shrink the suit down to a tiny size. He kicked it into the hand of Dib's mech and Dib launched it into space.

 

Out in space, it gathers power and returns every Christmas, which is why people live in a protective dome 2 million years later.

Sgt. Hobo 678

 

Major Appearances: Hobo 13

Quote: "Prepare yourselves, you slime licking smort-crabs, for a series of trials!"

Affiliation: Space- Hobo 13

See also: Hogulus

An instructor on military training planet Hobo 13, Hobo 678 has trained many aliens from across the Universe. He has a mechanical eye and arm. Major Appearances: FBI Warning of Doom

Quote: "Sergeant Slab Rankle, serial number F305769 and you are in direct violation!"

Affiliation: Mall

See also: Sgt. Shriver, Zombie Soldiers

Slab Rankle works as a mall security guard who takes his job a little too seriously. He has installed the mall with security that is the finest in Earth technology, and if you break a rule you get dropped into a prison pit until he selects you to be turned into a zombie soldier. When Zim bested even his zombie soldiers, Slab offered Zim to rule the mall with him. Zim declined.

Shadowhog

 

Major Appearances: Gaz, Taster of Pork

Quote: "As punishment, you must clean my filthy toilet of filthy piggy filth!"

Affiliation: Realm of the Shadowhog

See also: Pig Guards

A pig demon from another realm, the Shadowhog grants the mystical "sense of the shadowhog" for Gaz when Dib uses a spelldrive to conjure him. This spell makes everything Gaz eat taste like pork. Gaz gives Dib a chance to reverse the spell before she destroys him, and this leads them to the Shadowhog himself. The Shadowhog puts Gaz through a series of trials which she easily passes. Dib, not knowing how easily his sister passed, admits that he cast the spell for selfish reasons and requests to be punished. The Shadowhog makes Dib clean his toilet.

Shnooky

 

Major Appearances: Plague of Babies

Cameos: Tak, the Hideous New Girl

Quote: "Eat his feet off!"

Affiliation: Space- Nhar-Gh'ok

See also: Noogums' Mom, Private Fooby

Shnooky is a leader of the Nhar-Gh'ok. He was stranded on Earth when the Nhar-Gh'ok mother ship accidentally beamed aboard human babies when the Nhar-Gh'ok soldiers were ready to return from their information gathering mission. Shnooky was forced to take the identity of Noogums, a human baby who was beamed aboard the mother ship. Seven years later, Zim became Shnooky's neighbor. Shnooky witnessed Zim without his disguise and led the Nhar-Gh'ok soldiers into Zim's base with the intension of taking Zim's voot cruiser. Currently, Shnooky is now as stupid as a human baby because Zim used a power amplifier to send waves of stupidness from GIR into the Nhar-Gh'oks.

SIR units

 

Major Appearances: The Nightmare Begins -- MegaDoomer

Cameos: Planet Jackers

Quote: "Yes, master! I obey!"

Affiliation: The Irken Empire

See also: Invaders, Tak, MiMi, GIR

Standard-issue Information Retrieval units, known as SIRs, are issued to the Invaders before they are sent on their missions. SIRs are competent robot minions that can compact (and even serve as a thermos). Instead of wasting a SIR on ZIM, the Tallest built GIR out of spare parts and junk. Tak also has a SIR named MiMi, probably built from spare parts on Planet Dirt.

 

The Tallest kept a storage room full of malfunctioning SIRs that they intended to send to ZIM. They were accidentally sent to Invader Tenn and ravaged her base.

Sizz-Lorr

 

Major Appearances: The Frycook What Came From All That Space

Quote: "I'm coming, Zim! No use running!"

Affiliation: Shloogorgh's Flavor Monster

See also: Gashloog

A massive Irken who serves as the Frylord of the restaurant Shloogorgh's Flavor Monster on planet Foodcourtia, Sizz-Lorr was Zim's boss while he was exiled there. Zim left when he found out about Operation Impending Doom II and Sizz-Lorr later hunted him down, delayed by a time warp known as the Foodening. Sizz-Lorr brings Zim back to Shloogorgh's with new security measures installed to ensure he won't escape again.
