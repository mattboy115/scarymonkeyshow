Aimy

 

Aimy's biggest moment was in MegaDoomer, where she left her computer to investigate the noise of ZIM's invisible battle mech.

Alexi Aaronovich

 

"Submissions for next week's show!"

Alexi is a Zim crew cameo of character designer Aaron Alexovitch. Although his character had multiple jobs in the world of ZIM, Alexi is most notable as the assistant at the Mysterious Mysteries studio.

Brent Crowe

 

Brent Crowe is a cameo of a ZIM production coordinator. He can be seen boarding the bus in front of ZIM in Walk of Doom.

Carol Millican

 

Carol is designed after a director from Nickelodeon. She is the biology teacher in Dark Harvest and was responsible for moving Filler Bunny to the window sill, which resulted in his decapitation. Other moments include picking her nose in Walk of Doom, and getting crushed in Walk for your Lives.

Courtney Lilly

 

Courtney Lily is a cameo of a writer for ZIM. He got to walk the Spooky Chihuahua in MegaDoomer, but his greatest moment is by far in Battle of the Planets. Courtney has an extended scream as the planet Mars slowly crushes his can of Poop Cola.

Da' Cone

 

"Hey! Where's my free power, huh?"

Da' Cone is a very vocal stock character, so he's in the regular bios as well. He stargazed in Planet Jackers, complained in Future Dib, got raisen ice cream in Mortos Der Soulstealer, and was stolen by a hobo in Gaz, Taster of Pork.

Eric Trueheart

 

"I dunno, but don't eat the muffins."

Eric Trueheart is a cameo of a ZIM writer (and the lord of pants). Eric warned Dib about the muffins while hanging out with the crowd there to see Moofy in The Girl Who Cried Gnome.

Firgile

 

"I like burritos, but they sure don't like me."

A very large man, Firgile was featured in a diarrhea commercial. Someone also tried to cram him into a manhole to hide from Mars in Battle of the Planets.

Frank Conniff

 

"Yup, told ya he'd come."

Frank Conniff is a cameo of a ZIM writer, as well as a former writer from Mystery Science Theater 3000. He was seen as a saucer moron who prophesized the coming of ZIM. He was also one of the people hiding from the fund raising children in Door to Door, as well as a victim of Ultra-Peepi in Hamstergeddon.

He Eats Potatoes

 

He Eats Potatoes is the customer at The Krazy Taco who sniffs his burrito in disgust.

Heathe

 

Also known as XTRO, Heathe is a cameo of ZIM storyboard artist Chris Graham. If you look hard, you can see him shedding a tear after his brother Ian is crushed in The Most Horrible X-Mas Ever.

Hugh Monstro

 

"Hey! That kid's throwing punch!"

A prominant stock character (so he's in the normal bios too), Hugh yelled at Dib fro throwing punch in Parent Teacher Night. He also sold meat in Walk for your Lives and was a reporter in Dib's Wonderful Life of Doom.

Humph

Ian Graham

 

Ian (also known as 45 due to his hat) is a ZIM storyboard artist cameo. His biggest moment was in The Most Horrible X-Mas Ever, where he was crushed by a piece of debris from the teleporter.

Jay Bondy

 

Jay Bondy is a cameo of a color director for ZIM. He got covered in mud in Invasion of the Idiot Dog Brain and tripped on his doorstep in MegaDoomer.

Jim

 

Jim can be seen washing windows in The Wettening, before water washes him away.

Jhonen Vasquez

 

Jhonen Vasquez, the creator of ZIM, made cameos in some of the earlier episodes. He was one of ZIM's disguise candidates in The Nightmare Begins ("too ugly!") and sat with Steve Ressel in Mc Meaties in Germs. We get a glimpse of him sitting at home in The Wettening, where he chokes on a fish.

Jmily

 

"Who does your hair?"

Jmily speaks in one of the commercials GIR watches in Parent Teacher Night.

John Fountain

 

John borrows his character name from a ZIM character designer, but his body is designed after Richard Hatch from the show Survivor. He can be seen holding another man's hand in Walk of Doom, and was inspected by ZIM in Germs.

Kenny

 

Kenny is a tow truck driver with massive girth. In Dibship Rising, after Dibship knocks over a skool bus, Kenny single-handedly rights the bus. He then hooks it up to his truck and drives off.

Kyle Menke

 

Kyle is a cameo of a ZIM storyboard artist who boarded most of the awesome action sequences. Kyle swerved his car (based on his actual car, the "Dragon Wagaon") to avoid hitting Dib in Walk for your Lives, causing a massive pile-up.
