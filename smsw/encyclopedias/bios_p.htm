Peepi

 

Major Appearances: Hamstergeddon

Quote: none

Affiliation: Skool

See also: Ultra-Peepi

Peepi is the hamster-class skool pet brought in by Ms. Bitters to prepare the children for a life in the nightmarish corporate system. Zim recognizes how his cuteness effects the other students and decides to use this in a plot for world domination. He hooks up a throbbing alien device to transform the hamster into a giant beast. See Ultra-Peepi for more.

2 million years in the future, a group of children who live in a protective dome that keeps Santa out listen to Mr. Sludgy's stories. One of them questions aspects of the story, but Mr. Sludgy shoves that kid under the bed.

PresidentMan

 

Major Appearances: Dib's Wonderful Life of Doom -- The Girl Who Cried Gnome -- The Most Horrible X-Mas Ever

Quote: "You! Get me the nuclear people on the phone! I want those vibrations!"

Affiliation: President Land

See also: Moofy, Santa suit, PresidentMan's Announcer

The leader of the USA who hails from President Land, PresidentMan deals with important issues such as Moofy the girly ranger getting stuck in Zim's lawn and the return of Santa. His decisions have been known to be influenced by offerings of chocolate covered ninja star cookies. He was quick to give up all of his power to Santa upon his return.

PresidentMan's Announcer

 

Major Appearances: The Girl Who Cried Gnome

Quote: "All the way from President Land, it's PresidentMan!"

Affiliation: President Land

See also: PresidentMan

A man whose sole purpose appears to be announcing for PresidentMan, the announcer has a microphone helmet and picture of the President strapped to his chest.

Principle

 

Major Appearances: The Voting of the Doomed

Cameos: Tak, the Hideous New Girl

Quote: "You will obey! You won't complain about nasty toilets!"

Affiliation: Skool

See also: Student President, Willy

The skool principle first appeared as a voice over Ms. Bitters' phone informing her about the decision to celebrate Valentine's Day. He later appeared as a cloaked figure who took in Willy as the new Student President and had his mind altered. The principle carries a pet rodent.

Prisoner 777

 

Major Appearances: Backseat Drivers From Beyond the Stars

Quote: "That would be wrong! It'd infuriate the Armada!"

Affiliation: Space

See also: Lard Nar

Zim keeps in contact with a Vortian on the conquered planet Vort, now a Military Prison Planet. Prisoner #777 gives stuff to Zim, including the plans to the Massive (which he helped design), instructions on how to make a control seat, and the Vortian death weapon used in MiniMoose.

Private Fooby

 

Major Appearances: Plague of Babies

Quote: "You won't believe it, sir!"

Affiliation: Space- Nhar-Gh'ok

See also: Shnooky

Private Fooby is one of the Nhar-Gh'oks who took part on the information gathering mission on Earth. He wears a bunny suit, even before he was forced to take the identity of a baby. He was the Nhar-Gh'ok who discovered the human maternity ward in the hospital, leading the Nhar-Gh'oks to believe the humans were holding Nhar-Gh'oks hostage. When hospital staff nearly discovered the Nhar-Gh'oks, he hailed the mother ship but gave the wrong coordinates. They beamed up the babies in the maternity ward instead.

Professor Membrane

 

Major Appearances:

Quote: "Shtshtshtsht, not now son. I'm making... TOAST!"

Affiliation: Membrane Labs

See also: Dib, Gaz

Dib's father is a world-renowned scientist. He has a popular kids show called "Probing The Membrane Of Science." He is always working on new inventions and is hardly ever around. Professor Membrane's last name IS NOT MEMBRANE. His wife suffered a horrible fate and now her remains live in a jar somewhere.

Professor Membrane Devotee

 

Major Appearances: Battle-Dib

Quote: "That's not it! It's-"

Affiliation: Skool, Membrane Labs

See also: Audience Admissions Bot

One of the people taking the audience admissions test to "Probing the Membrane of Science" failed and was sent screaming out the back. She was decked out in full Prof. Membrane gear, including Prof. Membrane shaped hat, shirt, and more. She failed the test by 0.1%.

Professor Membrane Studio Guards

 

Major Appearances: Battle-Dib -- Future Dib

Cameos: Career Day

Quote: "Next time, we'll call your parents! Or legal guardian!"

Affiliation: Membrane Labs

See also: Professor Membrane, Gaz, Dib, Shunk Wugga, Future Dib

Professor Membrane has a couple of guards armed with electric taser-staffs. The skinny guard shocked Dib repeatedly when he tried to enter the studio. He later threw out Dib, along with the bulkier guard. Gaz was partnered with the skinny guard for Career Day. The black guard was tricked by Future Dib.
