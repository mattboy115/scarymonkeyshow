About Aleens

Here's some of the known information on Ratts Tyerell's species, the Aleena:

The Aleena live on the planet Aleen, located in the inner rim. There they have established themselves as a warrior race. The Aleena are littered with differences within their species, as they breed for variation- some have long necks, some have tails, some are all white... They come in different sizes, from 1 foot tall all the way to 6 feet and maybe more. Aleena have incredible reflexes due to quick change metabolism. They evolved to have such quick reflexes while evading the Sagcatchers, a predator native to Aleen. Aleen is also inhabited by Scatalpens, which slit the belly of their prey causing them to trip on their own entrails.
