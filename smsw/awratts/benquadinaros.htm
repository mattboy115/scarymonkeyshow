Ben Quadinaros arrived on Tatooine a few weeks before the Boonta Eve Classic. The Toong home world of Toong'l had a poisoned environment due to recent comet strikes, so Ben and his large family were forced to leave. They went to Tatooine after first spending time on Tund. Ben Quadinaros was a competitor in the Pouffra Circuit, a tamer podracing competition for amateurs. While at the Poodoo Lounge on the night before the Boonta, Ben attracted the attention of glimmik singer and Boonta competitor Boles Roor. Roor bet 5 million peggats (Podracing Tales says Wupiupi) that no Toong was brave enough to enter the Boonta. Ben took that bet, much to Boles Roor's surprise. He entered the race with a hastily built rental pod. At the start of the race, Ben Quadinaros' pod stalled along with Anakin. Later, the power couplings failed (due to Ark Roose's sabotage, who had thought that Ben Qaudinaros' pod was actually Anakin's pod), sending the engines flying off into different directions. Although Ben didn't even get past the starting grid, he got all the money he wanted from Boles Roor's bet.
