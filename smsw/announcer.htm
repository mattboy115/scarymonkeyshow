Announcers

 

Appearances: The Nightmare Begins, The Frycook What Came From All That Space

It is not known whether or not the Conventia Announcer is an Irken. He does have the green skin and teeth, but many of the alien species in Invader ZIM have similar traits to the Irkens. This entry only applies if the Conventia Announcer is indeed an Irken.

 

Irken announcers are able to transmit their messages through a legion of hover screens. They are often used during large events to give instructions to the masses. The Conventia Announcer's different clothes and apparent tall height might be explained if he was a distinguished Irken given the privilege to announce at such an important event as the Great Assigning.
