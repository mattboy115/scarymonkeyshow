Name: Mary
 
Seat: 14 (previous appearances in seat 10 and 7)
 
quotes:
 
"GET AWAY FROM ME YOU... object of my unsurpassed worship!"
"And I think unicorns and dragons are pretty! So that's why I'm a fairy princess for Halloween!"
"It's after my candy!"
Mary has appeared in the class a lot without actually being a classmate. She appeared in the seat of Chunk when Ms. Bitters said that there was nothing where her head was prior to the Big Bang. She appears partnered with a plumber on career day. Mary was later seen jump-roping in the play ground. Zim used Pustulio to hypnotize her. She appeared in Ms. Bitters' class once again on Halloween in place of Sara. She was dressed as a fairy princess. Later, while she was out trick or treating, GIR attacked her and stole all of her candy.

Mary replaced Tae in Season 2, becoming a permanent part of the class.
