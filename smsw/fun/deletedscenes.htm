Deleted Scenes and Edits

 

While Invader ZIM has a considerably low amount of deleted stuff as compared to most other NICK shows (mainly because of Jhonen's willingness to fight the censors) there still is some bits and pieces that were cut from the show. Besides the ones listed here, some of the later episodes have explosions and general destruction edited. Most stuff is changed or deleted in script or storyboard form though. We know of most of these because of Steve Ressel, the director of the show. Thanks to Gim for getting a few for me too. Here's a short list of some of them:

 

The Nightmare Begins: 

    The Digestor- After Zim received his sandwich from the Tallest, he was originally supposed to prove his worthiness in a gladiator style fighting ring. Teleporter rings come down and encircle Zim, transporting him into the fight with Irken gladiator armor on. A huge door opens and the Digestor, a 40 foot monster, emerges. Zim gives the beast his sandwich and the Digestor runs away like a pleased child. The Zim crew decided to take this scene out for time and pacing.     Impending Doom I- Zim's rampage through Irk was originally supposed to last about 45 seconds, but was also slimmed down for time.

    Milk Carton- The picture on the milk carton in Dib's fridge was that of a small child along with the words "Find Me!" Nick didn't like this, and it was changed to the Rob Hummel is Meat message (with a picture of Rob) that you can see now. There is still a shot with the small child when it zooms in on Dib falling into the sink.

    Final chase- Zim and Dib's chase sequence was longer too.

 

Parent Teacher Night:

    Diarrhea- The color of the word 'diarrhea' in the ad that GIR watches was going to be brown. Nick didn't like this, so Jhonen changed it to a dripping blood red, which Nick was okay with.

 

Walk of Doom: 

    Welcome to Mexico- Not a huge change, but there was originally supposed to be an actual sign that said 'Welcome to Mexico' at the end of Walk of Doom. Nick said it was offensive to Mexicans (despite the fact that Jhonen is Mexican), so it was scrapped. To compensate, Jhonen made the Mexico scene very surreal.

 

Bestest Friend: 

    The Ending- In the original ending to Bestest Friend, keef falls off of the roof due to the squirrel but grabs onto a telephone wire and gets electrocuted. Nick saw this as 'imitatable behavior' and ordered it to be changed. One of the ideas for a different ending was to have Keef run after the squirrel into the street and get hit by a car, lodging himself onto the tire and spinning around as the car drove away. The ending they went with is similar to the original ending, except without the telephone wire.

 

Attack of the Saucer Morons: 

    The Initiation- Zim's initiation as a member of the Children of the Bright and Shinning Saucer was originally going to be shown. It would involve the characters of Desmond Flapp, Trudy, Boll, and Yoa (the speaking saucer morons). It was cut for time.

 

Rise of the Zitboy: 

    Messing with their heads- Zim was supposed to get more usage out of his hypno-slaves. He forces one child to eat anti-matter, while another child is forced to pinch Ms. Bitters. He walks off screen and can be heard getting slapped.

 

Plague of Babies: 

    Extended opening- More stuff was going to happen before the episode started, such as the Voot Cruiser flying into a Poop Cola billboard.

 

Game Slave 2: 

    IGGIIIIIINS!- Iggins was originally supposed to die. But now, he comes back with his SUPER HUMAN STRENGTH.

 

Door to Door: 

    Cocktail roaches- The cocktail weenies the Grout children eat were originally supposed to be cockroaches.

    Virtual reality- Part of the VR sequence was changed after the episode was made, replacing destruction with monsters that enslave society. Both versions of the episodes have aired, and both versions can be read in the transcript section.

 

FBI Warning of Doom:

    Credits- The credits for Intestines of War had sexually explicit names in the storyboard for that episode (Jenny Talls and Uri Thra are two examples). Nick caught that and had it changed.

 

Battle of the Planets:

    Butt face- One shot that was cut was a view of Mars and Mercury coming together to look like a face going into a butt... or worse, if you imagine the shape of the control seat sticking out of the face on Mars.

    Flaming wreck- The wreck of a car during the panic scene was originally a flaming wreck.

 

Future Dib:

    Mushroom cloud- Future Dib's retelling of Zim sabotaging the PEG generator originally had PEG exploding and creating a Mushroom cloud.

 

Abducted: 

    Exploration- Zim's exploration of the Abductors' ship lasted a little longer, with Zim clinging to one of the moving pods. Zim's weight causes the pod to swing and crack open on a catwalk. The pod then gets retracted into a valve where it is burned. Zim falls through the fire and is spit back out, falling through air while unconscious. His spider legs grab the edge of a catwalk and swing him onto it, then walk him into the next room where the Blob thing is. This cut scene was fully animated and cut for time and personal reasons (Jhonen wanted less action in this episode).

 

MegaDoomer:

    Havoc- One cut moment was fire and explosion sweeping cars away from a street.

 

Tak: The Hideous New Girl:

    Skool bus- When the Earth's magma is pumped out, a skool bus was supposed to be shown rolling into a chasm on the wrinkled Earth.

 

GIR Goes Crazy and Stuff:

    Happy all the time- Nick's color scheme was originally green and orange (Nickelodeon's colors), but was changed to purple and orange to be 'less mean.'

 

Walk for your Lives:

    Explosion- The explosion was supposed to look more fiery and red, but Nick thought it looked too real. The crew was forced to go back and rework all the scenes with the explosion, giving it a green glow

    Iggins' end- That cop was supposed to kill Iggins.

 

The Most Horrible X-Mas Ever:

    Sludgy's mistakes- Mr. Sludgy, the mechanical snowman, would've referred to Zim and Dib as "Zam" and "Doob" since details are often forgotten over the passing of time, but the Nick executives found this to be "confusing."

    45's death- 45 was going to be crushed by a piece of debris knocked into the crowd by the Santa Suit monster. The shot of the crowd scattering as 45 just stands there is still in the episode, but the actual impact has been cut.
