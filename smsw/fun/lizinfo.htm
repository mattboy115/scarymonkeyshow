Lizard Boy Information

 

Who is Lizard Boy?

 

Lizard Boy is one of the many kids who goes to Zim and Dib's skool. He does not belong to Ms Bitters' class so he only pops up in the background so often. He has only spoken once.

 

Why Lizard Boy?

 

I dunno. I like his strange appearance. Mostly I just like to support minor characters, and Lizard Boy is as minor as they come.

 

Other details

 

-Lizard Boy is shorter than the average students in Ms Bitters' class, so it can be assumed that he is a grade bellow them.

-Lizard Boy is sometimes seen with Flan, so they may be good friends.

-Most of Lizard Boy's appearances come from the first few episodes. He then disappears for a long while until the last couple episodes. His biggest inclusion is in Rise of the Zitboy where he gets the most screen time as well as his only line ("I'm in pain!").

-According to Career Day, Lizard Boy will grow up to be some sort of masked madman, as he is paired with the Serial Killer.

-One of the saucer morons looks like he could have been designed as an adult version of Lizard Boy... A Lizard Man, so to speak.
