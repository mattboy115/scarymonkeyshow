The Scary Monkey Games
 
 
 
 
With the power of Klik 'n' Play, I have started creating Scary Monkey Games. The first one will be a 20 minute episode, that includes monkey stares, growls, heavy breathing and more. Sit and watch through it. I dare ya. The next will just be a program that allows you to move your mouse around the monkey and have his eyes follow the moving mouse.
