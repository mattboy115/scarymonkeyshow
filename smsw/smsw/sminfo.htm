Who or what is the scary monkey?
The Scary Monkey Show is a show that is popular within the cartoon Invader Zim (airs 9:30 pm on nickelodeon), a show by comic book artist
Jhonen Vasquez. In invader Zim, Zim's idiotic robot sidekick GIR enjoys the Scary Monkey Show. It is a show starring a monkey. A scary monkey. A monkey who rarely does anything more than stare. Despite all that, the Scary Monkey Show is popular. In the electronics store, all of the Televisions display the Scary Monkey Show.

Here are all the times the monkey is seen throughout the Invader Zim episodes seen so far. More will be added as new episodes air.

1- Parent Teacher Night
    This is the first appearance of the Scary Monkey Show. While preparing to feed the robo-parents information with the video 'Proper Earth Parenting,' Zim attached them to many televisions that played various shows. GIR tried to decide which show was his favorite. One of the shows was the Scary Monkey Show. GIR hugged the monitor playing it while saying, "I love this show."

2- Walk of Doom
     Zim decides to take GIR into the city and get lost to test the new guidance chip. GIR doesn't want to leave though because the Scary Monkey Show is on.
GIR: "Do we have to go right now? I wanna watch the Scary Monkey Show!"
Zim: "That monkey..."
     While getting lost in the city, Zim and GIR walk past a television store with TVs playing the Scary Monkey Show in the display window. The monkey is breathing heavily which adds even more creepiness to an already scary monkey. GIR stops to watch but Zim pulls on his leash.

3- Bestest Friend
     When Zim shuts the door on Keef, he sees GIR watching the Scary Monkey Show. Suddenly, the scary monkey growls, sending the amount of scary monkey quotes from zero to 1. That's an increase of infinity percent.
     "Grrrrr...."

4- Battle-Dib
      The scary monkey is seen with the top of his head removed displaying its brains on the opening of Prof. Membrane's show, "Probing the Membrane of Science."

5- Hamstergeddon
      GIR watches the angry monkey, which is the same monkey from the scary monkey show.

6- Invasion of the Idiot Dog Brain
      The Scary Monkey Show is on one of the floating monitor screens that GIR unleashes to 'cheer Zim up.' He is picking his nose.

7- Plague of Babies
      GIR watches the Scary Monkey Show while he's supposed to be panicking. Zim yells at him, and he goes back to screaming. This is the second time the scary monkey growls, and the second time the Scary Monkey Show is accompanied by music (the first being Hamstergeddon).

8- FBI Warning of Doom
       The scary monkey is in a prison cell underneath the mall along with other victims who broke the mall rules. The scary monkey growls and screeches in this episode. When the prisoners form a pyramid to make it out, the scary monkey is at the top of the pyramid.
 
9- Walk for your Lives
       When the city is being evacuated and Dib is trying to reach the police man, he sees several things flash by. One of them is two people stealing a TV that plays the Scary Monkey Show.
