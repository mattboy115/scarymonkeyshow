Lizard Boy Sightings

 

Here's a comprehensive list of all the times Lizard Boy can be seen, with links to screenshots.

Opening Credits

In one of the bumpers where Zim imagines a situation at the end of the opening credits, Lizard Boy can be seen jumping out of the way of a dodge ball.

Pic 1 Pic 2

Parent Teacher Night

1. Shows Flan how he can wiggle his tongue around inside a clear cup, which makes a strange sound.

Pic

2. Standing next to Flan as the Father Decoy runs backwards into the wall after the Mother Decoy hits him with the cup of punch.

Pic 1 Pic 2

Walk Of Doom
1. In the crowd of people watching the performers in the city as Zim and GIR perform.

Bestest Friend
1. Sits at a table in the cafeteria across from the table where the popular girls sit.
2. Gives a muffin to two students (one of them is Shiny) in exchange for their friendship.

Pic 1 Pic 2

3. Reads a book at a table with Shiny (quick pan).
4. In the playground, he stands by the jungle gym (wide shot).
5. Hangs upside down on the jungle gym.

Pic

6. Draws a picture of a jet when Zim and Keef show off their art.

Pic 1 Pic 2

7. He is standing in the hallway when Zim and Keef walk by.
8. Sits at a table in the cafeteria with Melvin, Tae, and The Letter M (wide shot).
9. Crumples up the invitation to Keef's surprise party and throws it at Keef.

Pic

10. In Zim's daydream, he is one of the children whose reflection can be seen on the glass of the tube Zim is imprisoned in.

Pic

11. In Zim's daydream, he is between two scientists in the circle of people dancing around Zim's tube in the next shot.

NanoZim
1. Waits in the lunch line of the cafeteria with a tray of food.

Pic

2. When Zim explains how he got in Dib's body, he is standing in the cafeteria holding his food tray.

Dark Harvest
1. When the classroom door opens up knocking Dib out of the way, he walks out behind Matthew P. Mathers III.

Pic

2. In the cafeteria on a bench with Flan, with his head tilted back (wide shot).

Attack of the Saucer Morons
1. On the bus that almost gets hit by the voot carrier. Carl sits in front of him, Sara sits behind him (quick pan).

Pic

The Wettening

Pic

Career Day

Pic

Pic 1 Pic 2

Battle-Dib

Rise of the Zitboy (the mother load!)
1. Lizard Boy is on the playground structure picking his nose. When he is hypnotized, he falls from the playground structure. After falling from the playground equipment and being covered in other children, he speaks for the first time!  "I'm in pain!" He is voiced by Jhonen himself, despite the fact that he is credited as being voiced by Rosearik Rikki Simons (GIR's voice).
