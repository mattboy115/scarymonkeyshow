How do you set them as your cursors once you've downloaded them? First, go to your control panel under my computer. Then, click on the icon entitled 'mouse.' Go to pointer, and then customize. Select which pointers you want to replace with the scary monkey and hit browse. Go to where you saved the scary monkey cursors and select one for each pointer you want to replace.
