Zita sits right behind Zim. According to the results of her blotch test, she is going to be a Nurse. When Peepi was made the class pet, Zita commented on how cute it was in a smurfette-type voice. She made fun of Zim when he had bologna on his face and she was disgusted by Dib's sandwichy smell. On Halloween, Zita dressed up as a space being. Zita was responsible for having Dib committed to the Crazy House for Boys. The Mysterious Mysteries Anchor interviewed Zita to get her opinion on Dib (she called him crazy). Zita got an A in class when she insulted Dib.
