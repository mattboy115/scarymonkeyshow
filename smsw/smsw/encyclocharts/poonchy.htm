Name: Poonchy, Drinker of Hate
 
Seat: 2
 
quotes:
 
"That's a stinkin' muffin!"
"But we're not asleep right now!"
"Hey! Dib's bein' all weird and giant again!"
"He likes wearing pants, Dib! Aliens don't like wearing pants!"
"Hey... Dib's got a BIRD DOODY ON HIS JACKET!"
Poonchy was first in line to purchase a Game Slave 2 at the Video Pit. He pranced and squealed when he got it. He was also the first to be hooked up to the pulley system and was sent flying out of the mall by Peaches. Poonchy can be seen in a inflatable swimming pool eating a Deelishus Weenie when the weenie stand transformation occurs.
 
After Brian was sent to the 'underground classrooms,' Poonchy took his seat for Season 2. When Sizz-Lorr came to the skool for Zim, he shoved Poonchy and his desk out of the way.
 
In the cancelled episode Invader Poonchy, (see RWaM's closet to view the episode's script) Zim draws Dib's attention away from himself by claiming that Poonchy is the real invader.
