Name: Gretchen
 
Seat: 17
 
quotes:
 
"I've never been to a party before."
"What are you talking about?"
"Why am I here?"
"Why are you telling me this?"
"Can I go now?"
"I really don't wanna be here!"
"This place smells, can I have a soda?"
"She does this, she just keeps talking."
Gretchen wears huge braces on her hideous teeth. She sits at the reject table during lunch. She was one of Zim's possible choices for a best friend. She failed all the tests and was left in a pitiful-looking state. She recovered within the next couple of days and attended Keef's "surprise party." On Halloween, she dressed up as Abe Lincoln. When the skool got lice, Gretchen was in the room with Countess Von Verminstrasser while she explained her plans. The Countess took Gretchen with her when she went chasing after Dib and Melvin. When they all ended up in the Lice Queen's hive, the lice queen assaulted them but couldn't even chip Gretchen's teeth. Gretchen has a crush on Dib and gave him a lot of Valintine's Day meat. She cried when she saw Dib and Tak talking.
