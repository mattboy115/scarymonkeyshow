By't Distombe

Classification Twi'lek
Gender Male
Height unknown
Home World Ryloth
Podracer unknown

By't was a competitor hoping to make it into the Vinta Harvest Classic on Malastare. During the 5th elimination race, Sebulba tossed a bottle of Tradium-brewed Vinta Harvest Ale at him. He lost control of his pod and flew over a gas stream, causing his cockpit to explode.
