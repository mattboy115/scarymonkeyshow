The Invaders

 

-Irken Biology- -Irken Society- -Irken Classes/Jobs- -Irken Military Vehicles- -Invaders-

 

A prestigious branch of the Irken Elite, only a few of the best soldiers are chosen to become Invaders. They are an important part of galactic conquest, as they serve as scouts to spy on an enemy planet. They blend in with the natives of that planet and collect data (with the help of a SIR unit) on how it would be best to conquer that planet. Once the planet is weakened, they call in for the Irken Armada to finish off the job and claim the planet as Irken territory. Here are all of the Invaders. Since most of them haven't done anything except stand around, many of the bios for them don't have anything to say.
