Abductors

 

Major Appearances: Abducted

Quote: "We shouldn't of fused him! We made him twice as powerful! Twice as deadly!"

Affiliation: Space

See also: Blob

The abductors are a father-son alien duo who travel through the galaxy collecting specimens in a massive holding area of their vast ship. However, they are so incompetent that 99.9% of their prisoners escape. Zim and GIR were their choice for a human and a dog, as the Abductors were fooled by their ingenious disguises, but they easily escaped. They kidnapped Zim and GIR by appearing at their doorstep in hideous human disguises, with the nametags Mary and Fred (but with the genders not matching the names). When Zim saw through their disguises, they stuffed the alien into a sack and beamed aboard their ship. Dib was later selected as their specimen of the Earth weasel. The two aliens (sometimes dubbed Blue and Green by fans due to their eye colors) are the same ones from Jhonen Vasquez's comics who abduct Rob Hummel and attempt to abduct Squee, except here they don't have their hover pod wheel chairs.

Administration Droid

 

Major Appearances: The Voting of the Doomed

Quote: "Do not be alarmed. The student president has experienced a failure in spirit."

Affiliation: Skool- administration

See also: Student President, Willy, Principle

When the student president collapsed during a pep rally, floating robots where unleashed into the skool to clean up the situation. An administration droid selected Zim and Willy as candidates for the new student president and later oversaw the process of the competitions.

Agent Darkbootie

 

Major Appearances: Battle of the Planets -- Zim Eats Waffles

Quote: "Let's just say my eyes are swollen!"

Affiliation: Swollen Eyeball Net

See also: Agent Disembodied Head, Agent Nessie

A member of the swollen eyeball net, Agent Darkbootie is actually a janitor at NASAPLACE. He helps Dib reach Mercury with an old monkey ship he repaired himself.

Agent Disembodied Head

 

Major Appearances: Zim Eats Waffles

Quote: "Agent Mothman? Stop callin' us!"

Affiliation: Swollen Eyeball Net

See also: Agent Darkbootie, Agent Nessie

Another member of the swollen eyeball net, Agent Disembodied Head has a bird perched on his shoulders. Dib contacts him while trying to show off footage of Zim but is disregarded as a prankster.

Agent Nessie

 

Major Appearances: Zim Eats Waffles

Quote: "A dog... eating waffles. Ha stinkin' ha!"

Affiliation: Swollen Eyeball Net

See also: Agent Darkbootie, Agent Disembodied Head

Agent Nessie is one of the swollen eyeball net members who Dib contacts when he tries to show off footage of Zim through live camera feed. Unluckily for Dib, Agent Nessie only sees Zim and GIR eating waffles.

Agent Tuna Ghost

 

Major Appearances: Gaz, Taster of Pork

Quote: "Agent Mothman, why were you talking out loud to yourself just then?"

Affiliation: Swollen Eyeball Net

See also: Shadowhog

Agent Tuna Ghost is the swollen eyeball net member who Dib contacts in order to help solve his problem with the "Sense of the Shadowhog" spell. She discusses the situation with the swollen eyeball net and figures out a way for Dib to be transported to the Shadowhog's realm by escaping to Mystical Hill.

Alan

 

Major Appearances: Dib's Wonderful Life of Doom

Quote: "What's left to be discovered now, Dib?"

Affiliation: Television, Holographic Creations

See also:

A talk-show host, Alan appears at the draining of Lake Spooky. Dib later appears on his show when he is an old man. Alan asks Dib if he threw the muffin at Zim. When Dib says 'yes,' Alan's head becomes Zim's head, and then Dib realizes his life with the Meekrob super-powers had all been a holo-simulation made by Zim to discover if he had thrown the muffin at him.
