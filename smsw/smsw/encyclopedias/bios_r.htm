Rat People

 

Major Appearances: Game Slave 2

Quote: "No way out! There's no way out!"

Affiliation: Mall

See also:

While looking for El Chupacabra and an exit from the parking garage, Dib stumbles onto a colony of horrible rat people. They claim they got lost too and eventually turned into rat creatures, and now they scourge the parking lot for food.

Receptionist

 

Major Appearances: Dark Harvest

Quote: "Oh, the green child! He's right over there doubled over in pain."

Affiliation: Skool

See also: Skool Nurse

The receptionist sits behind a desk in the waiting room of the Skool Nurse's office. She wastes the hours away by playing with a Moo Can. She informs Dib that no one has come in with head pigeons but directs him to the hall monitor, who is green from loosing his liver. She gets her brain replaced with a Poop cola can after Dib leaves, leaving her unable to talk but instead just ooze soda fizz from her mouth.

Rescue Workers

 

Major Appearances: The Girl Who Cried Gnome

Quote: "Goodness gracious, let me get you out of there!"

Affiliation: The government

See also: Moofy

When Moofy got her foot stuck in ZIM's gnome field, Rescue Workers were sent in to try and save her. They tried all kinds of methods, including being lowered in via helicopter or distracting Moofy with a doll. None of them could get close enough to her. After ZIM pulled Moofy free, the Rescue Workers left by rocketing into the sky.

 

One Rescue Worker has a smaller man peeking out of his chest.

Rob Hummel

 

Major Appearances: FBI Warning of Doom

Quote: "If that thing isn't in the drop off box by the time we open tomorrow, you're gonna payyy..... late fees!"

Affiliation: Mall- Video Outhouse, Cast and Crew Cameos

See also: Slab Rankle, Robert Hummel

Rob Hummel is a bald man who works at the Video Outhouse. He repeatedly calls GIR commanding him to return his overdue copy of "Intestines of War." When Zim answers the phone, he thinks it is the FBI warning him. Hummel is one of many characters based on the Zim writer of the same name.

RoboDad

 

Major Appearances: The Nightmare Begins -- Parent Teacher Night -- Backseat Drivers From Beyond the Stars

Cameos: Rise of the Zitboy

Quote: "That was my squeezin' arm. They took my squeezing arm! Why my squeezing arm!?!"

Affiliation: Zim

See also: RoboMom

Zim created the parent decoys in order to seem like an ordinary skool child. Most of their time is spent locked in the closet and they are only brought out when needed. RoboDad is Zim's robotic father stand-in.

Robot Bee

 

Major Appearances: Tak: the Hideous New Girl

Quote: none

Affiliation: Zim- experiment

See also:

Zim owns a robot bee that he suspects Tak is after. When Tak leaves after her visit to Zim's house, the robot bee flies away through the large hole she leaves.

Robot Gopher

 

Major Appearances: The Girl Who Cried Gnome

Quote: none

Affiliation: Zim- experiment

See also: Moofy

Zim's creation meant to deal with solicitors, he releases a robot gopher on girly ranger Moofy. The gopher manages to get Moofy stuck in Zim's lawn by digging a tunnel underneath her foot. The gopher then launches itself into a portal in the sky after doing a little dance, although Zim doesn't remember programming it to do that. Zim sends out a robotic gopher again when Dib gets trapped in the lawn.
