The Admiral

 

The Admiral is a floating worm creature encased in a circular jaw. The Admiral is the nightmare creature that stops to look at Giggles when he trips and falls.

Bobby Joe Ray Billy Sue

 

Bobby Joe is a freaky looking nightmare monster with a thin body and eyes on stalks.

Bride of Mortïs

 

Bride of Mortïs resembles medusa with her snake-like hair and serpent-like body.

Cage Monster

 

This monster was the one who captured Zim. Its hollow chest is a cage and it used a mouth-tipped tentacle to suck up Zim.

Dancing Skeletons

 

After a swarm of nightmare bats flew out of the nightmare Skool, a crowd of dancing skeletons appeared for no particular reason.

Droogle

 

Droogle is a Tongue Walker, with a bundle of tongues for feet supporting a small, weak body.

Giggles

 

An eyeless creature with a huge toothy jaw, Giggles is the nightmare creature who trips and falls after Zim is captured.

Gordon Peterson and Gordon Peterson Jr.

 

The Petersons are skull faced creatures that leap around like gazelles.

Hummelflesh, Destroyer of Worlds

 

"We brought him for you! His neck flashes and everythang!"

Hummelflesh served as the leading nightmare monster until he brought back Zim instead of Dib and was condemned to the realm of eternal screaming and restlessness by Nightmare Bitters. He enjoys eating.

Nightmare Keef

 

Nightmare Keef is identical to regular Keef, as he is enough of a nightmare in the real world. Nightmare Keef can be seen locked up with other monsters in the nightmare version of the Crazy House for Boys.

Nightmare NNY

 

Nightmare NNY is a nightmare creature resembling Johnny the Homicidal Maniac as a silhouette.

Oculord

 

Oculord has a giant eyeball for a head attached to a humanoid skeleton body.

Oraface

 

Oraface has massive teeth. It looks vaguely like the skool child Gretchen from the regular world. Oraface screeches at Dib when he is first put into a cage at the nightmare version of the Crazy House for boys.
Ouchmaster 5000

 

The Ouchmaster has three heads on one body.

Pepé

 

Pepé is a nightmare creature with spider legs immerging from his head.

Poofter

 

Poofter resembles a squid combined with a jellyfish. A tentacle emerges from a hole on his head.

Puncher of Heads

 

Puncher of Heads is a bat-like creature with massive hands at the tips of his wings.

Ralphie

 

Ralfie stands on all fours and has a vertical mouth.

Simon Rikkis

 

Simon is a nightmare monster with no head and a face on his chest.

The Slobulators

 

The Slobulators are giant slugs with band-aids on their heads.

Straight Jacket Monster

 

A living straight jacket monster captures Dib when the Nightmare White Coats come to take him out of his cell.

Yolk

 

"He said we could destroy his friend. Then he threw a can at my head. It hurt."

When Hummelflesh brought Zim back instead of Dib, Nightmare Bitters sent Yolk to announce that if Dib didn't surrender, Zim would be destroyed. Dib didn't care and he threw a can at Yolk's head. Yolk went back to Nightmare Bitters and due to his failure he was condemned to the realm of eternal screaming and restlessness.
