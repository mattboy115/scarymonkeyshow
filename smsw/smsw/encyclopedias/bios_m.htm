MiMi

 

Major Appearances: Tak: the Hideous New Girl

Quote: none

Affiliation: The Irken Empire

See also: Tak

Tak has a custom SIR unit (probably built from pieces of actual SIRs she obtained during janitorial work on planet Dirt) named MiMi. MiMi has a large claw hand used for destruction. While on Earth, MiMi goes disguised as a cat. She is very agile in this state. Gaz obtains MiMi's SIR memory disk which is used to find out Tak's plan. GIR is later hooked up to the disk so he can operate MiMi remotely, causing MiMi to act crazy and wreck Tak's ship while she is in space.

MiniMoose

 

Major Appearances: The Most Horrible X-Mas Ever

Quote: "*squeak*"

Affiliation: Zim

See also:

Zim's new assistant intended to be the ultimate in sidekick technology, MiniMoose is full of high tech devices including a Vortian Doomsday device hidden in his Nubs of Doom. MiniMoose cannot speak; instead he lets out the occasional squeak. He mostly just hovers around, but he can be quite useful sometimes. He would have been a permanent addition to the cast if Zim wasn't cancelled, but now he is only in the episode: The Most Horrible X-Mas Ever.

Monkey

 

Major Appearances: Future Dib -- Lice

Quote: none

Affiliation: Zim, Skool

See also: Countess Von Verminstrasser

When Dib hacked into the robot Dib, Zim used a monkey as a defense. It was put in the cage with Dib and prevented him from pressing the key on his laptop that would allow Dib to break into robot Dib's vocal circuits. Dib eventually tricked the monkey and pressed the key, angering the monkey. The monkey then beat up Dib. The monkey was later seen picking lice off of Rob's head and eating them while the skool was delousing.

Moofy

 

Major Appearances: The Girl Who Cried Gnome

Quote: "Noooo! I'm gonna be on TV forever!"

Affiliation: Girly Rangers

See also: Greg and the Girly Rangers, PresidentMan

A member of the Girly Rangers, Moofy attempted to sell some cookies to Zim but got her foot trapped in a robot gopher hole. The media came to cover the event and Moofy enjoyed the attention. Zim attempted to use the situation to his advantage by talking PresidentMan into letting him use a machine that would shake Moofy free- but destroy the Earth as well. Dib turned the tables by making a deal with PresidentMan to get him to use a digging machine instead, which would likely expose Zim's base. Zim took matters into his own hands by simply yanking Moofy free.

This large moose lives in a perfectly white room with nothing else in it. The moose can eat walnuts with a vengeance. Zim sends a bus full of students into a wormhole headed for the room with the moose.

Mooshy and Spoopty

 

Major Appearances: Vindicated!

Quote: "I don't know what you're talking about, but if it'll make you happy, we have some super weapons lyin' around if you want 'em."

Affiliation: Space

See also: Mr. Dwicky

Dib and Mr. Dwicky lure Zim into the forest by sending him a Plookesian signal and offering him super weapons. In the middle of their encounter, two real Plookesians named Mooshy and Spoopty come, having also recieved the signal. They give Zim super weapons, take Mr. Dwicky with them, and leave Dib with nothing.

Mortos Der Soulstealer

 

Major Appearances: Mortos Der Soulstealer

Quote: "Mortos recharge faster when he look good. When Mortos look good, Mortos feel good."

Affiliation: Place Beneath the Overworld

See also:

A demon who visits Earth every thousand years, Mortos happens to come to a graveyard in Dib's city. Dib hires Mortos to steal Zim's soul. Mortos agrees, but spends most of his time recharging his spooky powers. After wasting his powers on turning carnival rides into monsters and giving Da' Cone a spooky ice cream, Mortos leaves having given Dib nothing but trouble.

Mr. Dwicky

 

Major Appearances: Vindicated!

Quote: "All the child-like wonder was ripped from my heart the day my foot got stuck in an escalader and aliens didn't come rescue me."

Affiliation: Skool- councilor

See also: Mooshy and Spoopty

The new skool councilor, Mr. Dwicky tells Dib that he believes him about Zim and agrees to help. He teams up with Dib in a plot to lure Zim into the forest using a fake Plookesian signal. While waiting for Zim, Dwicky reveals that he doesn't actually believe Dib but wanted to help in order to figure out Dib's problems. Zim finally arrives and Dwicky's world is turned upside down. More real Plookesians arrive named Mooshy and Spoopty and offer to take Mr. Dwicky with them. He goes, accidentally taking the camera used to film Zim with him.

Major Appearances: Parent Teacher Night -- Gaz, Taster of Pork

Quote: "And that, class, is why I'm so happy today! Innit that great? Huh? Yeah!"

Affiliation: Skool- Mr Elliot's class

See also:

A former student of Ms. Bitters, Mr. Elliot is the teacher of Gaz's class. He is usually bursting with joy, the exact opposite of his former teacher.


In the cancelled episode Simon Sez Doom!, Mr. Elliot worked as a volunteer at the orphanage where he recruited Zim and Dib.

Ms. Bitters

 

Major Appearances:

Quote: "Be quick! I can only survive so long in the sun."

Affiliation: Skool- Ms Bitters' class

The teacher in Zim and Dib's class, Ms. Bitters is a creepy old woman. She sometimes slithers around like a serpent or materializes out of shadows. She is intent on crushing the dreams of her students. She first appeared in Jhonen's Squee! comics as the teacher of Squee's class.

Mysterious Mysteries Anchor

 

Major Appearances: Mysterious Mysteries -- The Sad, Sad Tale of Chickenfoot

Quote: "I'd like to thank you all for watching what will almost certainly be the final episode of Mysterious Mysteries."

Affiliation: Mysterious Mysteries

See also: MM Producer, Alexi Aaronovich

The host of Dib's favorite show, Mysterious Mysteries. He refused to use any of Dib's submissions due to a mysterious run-in with Dib in the past resulting in a scar. The anchor locked Dib's many submissions away in a room entitled 'the Dib Archives' until the show was running low on content and he was forced to use a tape of Zim that Dib recorded.

Major Appearances: Mysterious Mysteries

Quote: "Hey, got bad news, you're fired! Hahaha, gotcha!"

A hefty lady, she threatened to fire the Mysterious Mysteries Anchor if he couldn't liven up the show. She enjoyed his fit of craziness at the end of the Zim videotape episode and wanted to see more like it in future episodes.
