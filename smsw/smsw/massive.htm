The Massive

 

The Massive truly lives up to its name with its gargantuan size. It is the Tallest's personal ship. Designed by Vortian engineers for Tallest Miyuki, The Massive is a bludgeon class vehicle that is the heart of the Irken Armada. The fuel pods contain Almighty Tallest Red and Purple's snack supplies. Captain Lard Nar lent a hand in the initial designs of the ship, while the Vortian who became Prisoner 777 finalized it.
